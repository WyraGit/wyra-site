What we do

Hardware, firmware and the math behind them

Embedded firmware

ESP32 (classic and C3) and Arduino: Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, displays, setup web pages and settings stored in flash.

IoT monitoring

Sensor networks that report JSON over MQTT, with network time and topics ready for Home Assistant.

Audio devices

Digital audio with I2S converters, streamed over TCP or received from phones over Bluetooth A2DP.

Scientific computing

Simulations and numerical methods in C++ and Python, data analysis and machine-learning prototypes.
